The spots

EVERY SPOT. EVERY POKÉMON.

Each spot comes with the Pokémon listed on it, and every spot is in play in every box. If a card featuring one of your Pokémon comes out of the vault, it’s yours. Tap a spot to see its full checklist.

Spots are not numbered in any particular order, and every spot can hit in any break. Checklists keep growing as cards that fit each box’s price range are added, including new releases.
